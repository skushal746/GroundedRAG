Orbitask Release Notes
======================

Version 4.3 (1 March 2026)
--------------------------
Billing: the refund window for annual plans has been extended from 14 days to 30 days. The
new window applies to annual purchases and renewals made on or after 1 March 2026.

New: Workload view shows each member's assigned tasks per week so managers can rebalance
work. Available on Business and Enterprise plans.

API: v1 is now officially deprecated. It will be shut down on 31 December 2026.

Version 4.2 (15 January 2026)
-----------------------------
Gantt view is now available on the Team plan. Previously it required Business or Enterprise.

The monthly automation run allowance on the Team plan increased from 2,500 to 5,000 runs.

Fixed: calendar view showed tasks one day early for workspaces in UTC-10 and UTC-11 time
zones.

Version 4.1 (20 October 2025)
-----------------------------
Recurring tasks support custom intervals such as "every 3rd weekday" or "last Friday of the
month".

Webhook deliveries are now retried up to 8 times (previously 5).

Fixed: ORB-4091 lock errors were returned too often when two automations updated the same
task at the same time.

Version 4.0 (4 August 2025)
---------------------------
API v2 is generally available, with cursor pagination and per-workspace rate limits.

Personal access tokens now expire after 90 days by default. Existing tokens created before
4 August 2025 keep their original expiry.
