# Orbitask Help Center: Frequently Asked Questions

_Last updated: 10 November 2025_

## Account and sign-in

### How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** and enter the email address you use for
Orbitask. We will send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. If you sign in with Google
or Microsoft single sign-on, reset your password with that provider instead.

### Can I turn on two-factor authentication?

Yes. Go to **Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication** and scan the QR code with an
authenticator app such as 1Password, Authy or Google Authenticator. Save the ten backup
codes somewhere safe; each code can be used once if you lose access to your phone.

### How do I change the email address on my account?

Go to **Profile > Account > Email**. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the
change only takes effect after you click it.

## Workspaces and members

### How do I transfer workspace ownership?

Only the current Owner can transfer ownership. Go to **Settings > Members**, open the menu
next to the new owner's name and choose **Make owner**. The new owner must already be an
Admin. The previous owner becomes an Admin.

### What can guest users do?

Guests can view and comment on the projects that are explicitly shared with them, and can be
assigned tasks in those projects. They cannot see other projects, create projects or invite
people. Guests do not count as paid seats.

### How do I remove someone from my workspace?

Admins and the Owner can go to **Settings > Members**, open the member's menu and choose
**Remove from workspace**. Tasks assigned to the removed person stay in place and are marked
as unassigned.

## Billing

### Can I get a refund?

Annual plans can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are non-refundable,
but you can cancel at any time and you will keep access until the end of the billing period.

### Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or schools?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited educational institutions are eligible for
discounted pricing. See the Billing & Refunds Policy for the current discount rates and how
to apply.

## Data and privacy

### How do I export my data?

Workspace Owners and Admins can go to **Settings > Workspace > Export** and choose CSV or
JSON. Large exports are prepared in the background; we email a download link when the file
is ready. Export links expire after 7 days.

### Does Orbitask work offline?

The iOS and Android apps keep a local copy of your tasks from the last 30 days, so you can
view and edit them without a connection. Changes sync automatically when you are back online.
If the same task was edited elsewhere in the meantime, the most recent change wins and the
other version is kept in the task's activity history. The web app requires a connection.

## Support

### How do I contact support?

Email support@orbitask.example at any time. Paid plans get email responses 24 hours a day,
five days a week (Monday to Friday). Free plan requests are answered on a best-effort basis.

### Is live chat available?

Live chat is available to Business and Enterprise customers, Monday to Friday from
08:00 to 20:00 CET. Open it from the **?** menu in the bottom-left corner of the web app.
Enterprise customers also get a named customer success manager.
